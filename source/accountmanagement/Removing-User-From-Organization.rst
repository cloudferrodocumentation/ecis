Removing user from Organization
===============================

This procedure removes a user from your organization in the portal.

After the user is unassigned, they no longer belong to your organization and will lose access to resources and views granted through that membership.

Prerequisites
-------------

No. 1 **Hosting account**

You need an active |brand-name| hosting account and access to the portal |brand-name-site-auth-link|.

No. 2 **Permissions**

You need sufficient permissions to manage organization members.

Procedure
---------

After logging in to the portal, open **Sub-accounts** from the left-side menu.

.. figure:: removing-user-from-organization-.png
   :align: center
   :class: image-with-border

The page shows the users currently assigned to your organization.

Select the user you want to remove, click **Unassign**, and then click **Confirm**.

Result
------

The user is removed from your organization.

The removed user receives a notification informing them that they have been unassigned from the organization.
